# davidmareshbook.com

Author website for **Dr. Robert David Maresh** and his memoir *Sins of the Father: How I Overcame the Effects of Incest—More or Less*.

Designed and developed by Artchelo Design for Olympus Story House.

## Structure
- `index.html`: the whole site as one self-contained file (Tailwind via CDN, images embedded). Pages are hash routes: `#/` Home, `#/author`, `#/book`, `#/reviews`, `#/contact`.
- `favicon.ico`, `favicon-32.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `favicon-512.png`: icons cut from the book cover.
- `docs/sitemap-and-copy.md`: sitemap and page copy.

## Notes
- The contact form is a front-end preview only; connect it to a mail handler before launch.
- Crisis-line numbers (988, RAINN) are included in the content note, contact page and footer. Confirm with the author/publisher before launch.
